Methodology | CRISP-DM Framework

Phases & Timeline

1. Business Understanding

Define KPIs: demand accuracy & revenue uplift; stakeholder interviews with SACCOS.

2. Data Understanding

GTFS historical schedules + weather/POI proxies; exploratory analysis of 135 routes.

3. Data Preparation

Cleaning missing timestamps, merging weather patterns, POI density per stop.

4. Modeling

Prophet, XGBoost, LightGBM, LSTM training; hyperparameter tuning.

5. Evaluation

Champion ensemble (XGBoost+LSTM) based on RMSE & MAE.

6. Deployment

Model serialization (pkl) & Streamlit prototype ready.

Model Comparison: Strengths & Weaknesses

ModelStrengthsWeaknesses
ProphetHandles seasonality & missing data; interpretableLess adaptive to sudden shocks; lower accuracy with high-dim features
XGBoostHigh performance, handles non-linearity, feature importanceRequires careful tuning; not native time-series sequential memory
LightGBMFast training, lower memory, leaf-wise growthCan overfit small datasets, less robust to noise
LSTMCaptures long-term temporal dependencies; sequential learningComputationally heavy; needs large data; less interpretable
Ensemble strategy: Weighted combination of XGBoost (demand peaks) + LSTM (temporal flow) yields best generalization across all 135 routes.
