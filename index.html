Machine Learning Driven Demand Forecasting & Dynamic Pricing for Nairobi Matatus

Okoth Joshua Jovern · JKUAT (Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology)

Digital Matatus GTFS · Weather & POI Proxies · 135 Routes

Abstract

This project leverages Digital Matatus GTFS data integrated with external proxies including Open-Meteo weather API and OSM Points of Interest (POI) to forecast passenger demand across 135 key matatu routes in Nairobi. Using an ensemble of time-series and gradient boosting models, we designed a dynamic pricing framework that responds to real-time demand fluctuations. The final solution demonstrates significant operational efficiency and revenue uplift potential for the informal public transport sector.
